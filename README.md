**Building the MinimalistiCE frame**

The table below shows all necessary components to build a full frame:


**Bill of Materials for the MinimalistiCE Frame**

| **Description** | **Quantity** | **Retail price per unit (USD)\*** |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 20×20 mm aluminium profile, 6 mm slot, 400 mm length | 4   | 2.96 |
| 20×20 mm aluminium profile, 6 mm slot, 200 mm length | 4   | 1.48 |
| 20×20 mm aluminium profile, 6 mm slot, 160 mm length | 2   | 1.18 |
| 20×20×17 mm aluminium corner bracket | 8   | 0.58 |
| Internal 90° L-connector for V-Slot | 8   | 0.55 |
| M4 hammer T-nut for 6 mm slot | 16  | 0.26 |
| M4 × 8 mm button-head Allen screw | 16  | 0.20 |

\* Average market prices for Brazil, taken as 1 USD = 5.4 BRL (24-nov-25)


The first step is to build the "side frame" twice, using **2x 400 mm profiles**, **2x 200 mm profiles** and **4 L-connectors**. Note that the intermediate profile should be set loosely, as it will be aligned with the high voltage power supply later in the build. **2x internal 90° L-connectors** should be installed in the lower joint of the intermediate profile and, as the aluminium bar itself, should also be set loosely.

The second step is to connect **2x 160 mm profiles** to one of the "side frames". An **internal 90° L-connector** should be installed in each of the formed joints.

The last step is to attach the other "side frame" to the structure and installing the final **internal 90° L-connectors** . A fully built, correctly assembled frame should look as follows:

<img width="500" height="500" alt="IV Pokéball teste v1" src="https://github.com/user-attachments/assets/617d95f1-4a60-43ec-a06d-5e4ae5669f15" />


Any bending or stress perceived in the structure can be remediated by loosening and subsequently tightening the appropriate joints.
