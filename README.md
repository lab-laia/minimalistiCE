**Building the MinimalistiCE structure**

The table below shows all necessary components to build a full frame:

**Bill of Materials for the MinimalistiCE Frame**

| **Item** | **Quantity** | **Description** |
| --- | --- | --- |
| A.2.a. | 4   | 20×20 mm aluminium profile, 6 mm slot, 400 mm length |
| A.2.b. | 4   | 20×20 mm aluminium profile, 6 mm slot, 200 mm length |
| A.2.c. | 2   | 20×20 mm aluminium profile, 6 mm slot, 160 mm length |
| A.2.d. | 8   | 20×20×17 mm aluminium corner bracket |
| A.2.e. | 8   | Internal 90° L-connector for V-Slot |
| \-  | 16  | M4 hammer T-nut for 6 mm slot |
| \-  | 16  | M4 × 8 mm button-head Allen screw |

The 20×20 mm aluminium, 6 mm slot profiles should contain a central hole, as shown:

<img width="200" height="200" alt="Image 1" src="https://github.com/user-attachments/assets/4f839a6a-7744-44b2-ba3a-052ab3a4649e" />

A threading tool must be employed to produce M5 taps in one end of all **4x 400 mm profiles**, as these connections will be utilized to attach the 3D printed base to the frame.

The first step is to build the "side frame" twice, using **2x 400 mm profiles**, **2x 200 mm profiles** and **4 L-connectors**. Care should be taken to align all 4x M5 threads downward. Note that the intermediate profile should be set loosely, as it will be aligned with the high voltage power supply later in the build. **2x internal 90° L-connectors** should be installed in the lower joint of the intermediate profile and, as the aluminium bar itself, should also be set loosely.

<img width="200" height="200" alt="Image 2" src="https://github.com/user-attachments/assets/cadd11a1-4651-4f48-ad85-27193c80daba" />

The second step is to connect **2x 160 mm profiles** to one of the "side frames". An **internal 90° L-connector** should be installed in each of the formed joints.

<img width="200" height="200" alt="Image 3" src="https://github.com/user-attachments/assets/ca59db08-8f34-43d1-b539-1d70fc0b5bb1" />

The last step for the structure itself is to attach the other "side frame" to the structure and installing the final **internal 90° L-connectors** . A fully built, correctly assembled frame should look as follows:

<img width="200" height="200" alt="Image 4" src="https://github.com/user-attachments/assets/75b19e2f-85bb-4e0f-b081-b258eea9b545" />

Any bending or stress perceived in the structure can be remediated by loosening and subsequently tightening the appropriate joints.

The following step is to install the **3D printed base** (.stl and .f3d files attached):
